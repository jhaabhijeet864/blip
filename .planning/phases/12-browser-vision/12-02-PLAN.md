    1. Create `extension/src/gestures/snap-kinematics.ts`:
       - State machine: `IDLE` -> `ARMED` (thumb-middle distance < 0.06 for >= 2 frames) -> `WAIT_AUDIO` (distance rapidly opens > 0.12).
       - If `onClickDetected` occurs within 200ms of finger release: fire `ON_SNAP_TRIGGERED`!
       - Implement Goku Instant Transmission detector:
         - Dual fingers (index tip id 8 and middle tip id 12) raised near forehead/temple.
         - Wrist positioned below face level, ring and pinky curled down.
         - Hold for >= 3 frames to prevent accidental triggers.
       - Enforce 1500ms debounce cooldown after any trigger.
    2. Create `extension/tests/gestures.test.ts`:
       - Verify visual pinch without audio click does NOT trigger snap.
       - Verify audio click without visual pinch does NOT trigger snap.
       - Verify visual pinch + audio click within 200ms triggers snap successfully.
       - Verify audio click arriving > 250ms late is rejected.
       - Verify Goku temple touch triggers instant transmission.
       - Verify 1.5s cooldown debounces subsequent triggers.
